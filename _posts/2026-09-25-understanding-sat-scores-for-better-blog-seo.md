- **Start with User Intent:** Answer common questions about SAT scores (e.g., “What is a good SAT score?”).
- **Use Data and Visuals:** Incorporate up-to-date statistics, charts, and infographics.
- **Optimize for Featured Snippets:** Structure your content to answer questions concisely.
- **Link to Trusted Sources:** Build credibility by referencing official SAT data and college board resources.
- **Regularly Update Content:** SAT policies and averages change, so keep your posts current.
