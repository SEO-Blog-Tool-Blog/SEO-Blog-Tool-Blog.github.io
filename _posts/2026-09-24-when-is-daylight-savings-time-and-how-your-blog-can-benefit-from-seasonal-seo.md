## Understanding Daylight Savings Time in North America

In most regions of the United States and Canada, daylight savings time begins on the second Sunday in March and ends on the first Sunday in November. This means clocks spring forward by one hour in March and fall back by one hour in November. These changes spark a surge in online searches as individuals and businesses look for reminders, tips, and information about the time shift.
