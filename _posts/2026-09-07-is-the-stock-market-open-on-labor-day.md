## Stock Market Hours on Labor Day in North America

In North America, both the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and NASDAQ are closed on Labor Day, which falls on the first Monday of September each year. This federal holiday is observed by financial institutions across the United States, meaning there is no trading activity on this day. Regular market hours resume on the following business day.
