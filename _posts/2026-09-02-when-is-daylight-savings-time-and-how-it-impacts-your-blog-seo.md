## Why Daylight Savings Time Matters for Blog SEO in North America

Daylight savings time in the United States and Canada typically begins on the second Sunday in March and ends on the first Sunday in November. In 2024, clocks spring forward at 2:00 a.m. on March 10 and fall back at 2:00 a.m. on November 3. This schedule impacts search trends and user activity across North America, as people look for information about time changes, clock adjustments, and related topics.
